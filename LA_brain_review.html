LA–Brain Exposure–Outcome Pair Matrix

Each cell is one exposure–outcome pair, coded from the study's fully adjusted model. The highlighted columns are the primary models. Hover or tap a cell for the estimates. Click any column name to sort; click again to reverse.

Associated: at least one fully adjusted test of that pair was significant (p<0.05). Null: no fully adjusted test was significant. Unadjusted: only unadjusted comparisons were reported. A “test” is each LA measure × outcome definition the paper reported in its fully adjusted model; the hover card lists every test, with significant ones in bold.

Infarct (primary models) is MRI-detected: silent infarcts, lacunes, cortical infarcts, microinfarcts, and acute infarct volume in stroke patients (Homssi). Clinical stroke is adjudicated ischemic stroke (incident, plus TIA in Habibi; prevalent prior stroke in Bianco). WMH includes WMH volume or load, white matter grade, Fazekas and ARWMC scores, and abnormal white matter volume. Cognition is neuropsychological test performance; Δ marks studies that also tested change over follow-up. Dementia is adjudicated incident dementia. DWI covers diffusion measures (fractional anisotropy, free water).

Mean age is in years (± SD). Infarct prevalence is the share of the analysed sample with ≥1 MRI-detected infarct, shown only for studies with an infarct outcome; Tan shows lacunes, Homssi enrolled only acute stroke patients, and Masini shows incident clinical ischemic stroke over follow-up. Hover a value for its definition.
